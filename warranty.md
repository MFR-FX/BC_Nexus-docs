**Publisher:** fx-its (Marco Frerix)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-13

## 1. Warranty Duration

fx-its warrants BC Nexus against material defects in its core functionality for a period of **90 days** from the date of initial installation in your Business Central environment (the "Warranty Period").

---

## 2. Scope of Warranty

During the Warranty Period, fx-its warrants that:

- BC Nexus will perform substantially in accordance with its published documentation and the feature descriptions in the AppSource listing.
- The extension will install and uninstall cleanly in a Business Central Cloud (SaaS) environment running the supported platform version stated in `app.json`.
- Critical defects that prevent the core functionality (Receive, Send, Publish) from working as documented will be investigated and, where technically feasible, corrected or mitigated.

---

## 3. Exclusions

This warranty does **not** cover:

- Issues caused by misconfiguration of endpoints, field mappings, or authentication settings.
- Incompatibilities caused by third-party extensions, custom AL code, or modifications made by anyone other than fx-its.
- Data loss or corruption resulting from customer-controlled integrations, external systems, or network failures.
- Features or behaviors not explicitly documented in the BC Nexus documentation.
- Business Central platform bugs, Microsoft SaaS outages, or changes to the BC platform that break previously working behavior.
- Issues arising from use of the extension outside the documented scope or on unsupported BC platform versions.
- Defects reported after the Warranty Period has expired.

---

## 4. Warranty Claim Process

To make a warranty claim during the Warranty Period:

1. Email **support@fx-its.de** with the subject line `[BC Nexus] Warranty Claim` (or use the ticket portal once it is live — see [Support]({{ site.baseurl }}/support/)).
2. Describe the defect and how it deviates from the documented behavior.
3. Include your installation date and the BC Nexus version (from the extension management page in Business Central).

fx-its will acknowledge warranty claims within **5 business days** and provide a resolution plan or workaround within **30 business days**, subject to technical feasibility.

---

## 5. Remedy

If a covered defect is confirmed, fx-its will, at its sole discretion:

- Provide a corrected version of the extension, or
- Provide a documented workaround, or
- Issue a refund of any purchase price paid (if applicable).

These remedies are the **sole and exclusive remedies** for any breach of this warranty.
