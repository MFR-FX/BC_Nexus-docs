## 1. Defect Remedy for the Entire Term

BC Nexus is licensed for a limited term against recurring fees. Under Section 7 of the [EULA]({{ site.baseurl }}/legal/eula/), fx-its keeps the Software in a condition suitable for the contractual use for the **entire term** of the license and remedies defects within a reasonable period. There is no separate warranty period: a defect reported in the third year is handled exactly like one reported in the first week. This page describes how to report a defect and what you can expect.

---

## 2. What Counts as a Defect

A defect is a deviation of BC Nexus from its documentation that more than insignificantly impairs its use, in particular:

- BC Nexus does not perform in accordance with its published documentation and the feature descriptions in the AppSource listing.
- The extension does not install, upgrade or uninstall cleanly in a Business Central online environment running a supported platform version.
- The core functionality (Receive, Send, Publish) does not work as documented.

When Microsoft releases a new Business Central version, fx-its adapts BC Nexus within a reasonable period. A malfunction caused by such a platform change is handled as a defect.

---

## 3. What Is Not a Defect

The following are not defects of BC Nexus:

- Issues caused by misconfiguration of endpoints, field mappings, or authentication settings.
- Incompatibilities caused by third-party extensions, custom AL code, or modifications made by anyone other than fx-its.
- Data loss or corruption resulting from customer-controlled integrations, external systems, or network failures.
- Behavior that the BC Nexus documentation does not describe.
- Business Central platform bugs and Microsoft service outages.
- Issues arising from use of the extension outside the documented scope or on Business Central versions that Microsoft no longer supports.

Questions about configuration and help with such issues are support requests, see [Support]({{ site.baseurl }}/support/).

---

## 4. How to Report a Defect

1. Email **support@fx-its.de** with the subject line `[BC Nexus] Defect: <brief description>` (or open a request in the [ticket portal](https://fx-its.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/1) — see [Support]({{ site.baseurl }}/support/)).
2. Describe the defect and how it deviates from the documented behavior.
3. Include the BC Nexus version (from the extension management page in Business Central) and the steps to reproduce.

fx-its acknowledges a defect report within **5 business days** and provides a correction or a workaround that restores the documented use within **30 business days**. For a defect that prevents the core functionality from working at all, fx-its starts the analysis within **2 business days**. These times are targets; they do not extend the reasonable period for remedying a defect in an individual case, which may be shorter depending on the severity of the defect.

---

## 5. Remedy

If a defect is confirmed, fx-its will, at its choice, provide a corrected version of the extension or a documented workaround that restores the documented use. Remedying defects is part of the license fee: it never counts against the support hours included in the license and is never billed separately.

Your statutory rights under rental law remain unaffected, in particular the reduction of the fees (Section 536 BGB) and termination for good cause if fx-its does not remedy a defect within a reasonable period (Section 543 BGB). Liability for damages is governed by Section 8 of the [EULA]({{ site.baseurl }}/legal/eula/).
